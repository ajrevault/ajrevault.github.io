Week 3: Policy Debate

France's Public Debt Struggles

France's public debt and deficit have now largely exceeded EU legal thresholds. Debt sat at 115.7% of GDP in 2025, nearly twice the 60% limit, while deficit reached 5.1%, also well above the 3% limit. While these numbers support a policy of fiscal consolidation, political disagreement on the best consolidation package, especially ahead of the 2027 presidential election, is likely to make any legislative agreement difficult.
